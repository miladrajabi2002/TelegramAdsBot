# Advertising Content Policy

- [Telegram Ad Platform Terms](https://ads.telegram.org/tos)

Support approval does not guarantee Telegram approval, and Telegram does not guarantee a fixed review time.

## 1. Ad format

In the Telegram Ads text format, an ad contains text and a button opening the destination. The maximum text length is 160 characters, including spaces.

The internal campaign title is for order management only and is not shown to the audience.

## 2. Editorial requirements

Copy must be clear, accurate and properly written. The following are not accepted:

- profanity, vulgarity or masked variations;
- meaningless, excessively vague or repetitive copy;
- gimmicky capitalization, spacing or symbols;
- Unicode art, ASCII art or box drawing;
- intrusive or excessive emoji;
- line breaks, bullets or numbered lists in ad copy; or
- unsupported absolute claims such as “best”, “highest income” or “guaranteed”.

## 3. Links

- The primary link belongs in the URL field.
- Copy may contain at most one optional link in an accepted format.
- The in-text link and button URL must lead to the same destination.
- A bot destination may use a valid start parameter.
- URL shorteners, IP-address links and deceptive links are not accepted.
- The destination type must be supported by the current Telegram Ads account and campaign format.

## 4. Destination quality

A promoted channel, bot or account must have a profile image and a complete About or Description.

The destination must:

- be active, complete and technically functional;
- contain original content relevant to the ad;
- be accessible to the targeted users;
- not be hidden behind an unrelated login, paywall, server error or under-construction page;
- not automatically redirect to an unrelated page or domain; and
- not exist primarily to collect data or display other advertising.

Bots must respond properly on mobile and desktop. Abandoned channels are not accepted and a channel must have at least one post during the two weeks before review.

Destination content must not be concealed or replaced to evade Telegram review.

## 5. Relevance and targeting

- The destination must match the claims in the ad.
- The ad, destination and target channel language must align.
- Keywords must not exploit harmful or sensitive topics as clickbait.
- For channel-targeted campaigns, target channels must be public and have more than 1,000 subscribers.

Telegram determines channel eligibility, inventory availability and final delivery.

## 6. Suggested channels

Suggested channels are listed for convenience only. A listing does not imply ownership, partnership or guaranteed delivery.

Do not choose targets solely by subscriber count. Relevance, language, activity and audience quality matter. Diversifying across relevant channels may reduce concentration, but no combination or outcome is guaranteed.

## 7. Impressions and CPM

An impression is a recorded display event. It does not necessarily represent a unique user, click, join or purchase.

CPM is the bid per one thousand impressions. A higher bid may improve auction opportunity but does not guarantee speed or results.

A frequency cap may be offered only when supported for the campaign type and operational account.

## 8. Prohibited content

These rules apply to ad copy, the promoted product or service and its destination.

### 8.1. Graphic, shocking or sexual content

- gore, injury, bodily fluids, accident or torture imagery;
- nudity or sexually explicit or suggestive content;
- sexual merchandise or entertainment; and
- dating services.

### 8.2. Hate, violence and harassment

- promotion of violence or support for violent organizations;
- bullying, degrading speech, defamation, threats or exposure of personal data; and
- discrimination based on race, religion, ethnicity, age, belief, orientation or ability.

### 8.3. Third-party rights

- unauthorized movies, music, books, software or other copyrighted material;
- impersonation of a person or brand;
- misleading use of another party's logo, trademark or assets; and
- privacy or personality-rights violations.

Using Telegram's name or logo to imply an official affiliation is prohibited.

### 8.4. Deceptive, misleading or predatory advertising

- clickbait, fake prizes and shock or fear tactics;
- unsupported absolute claims or comparisons;
- inappropriate references to a viewer's personal attributes, finances or religion;
- implying negative consequences to pressure a viewer;
- exploiting tragedy, health crises or mass violence; and
- concealing a paid product's price, fees, deadlines or material terms.

### 8.5. Political, sensitive and religious topics

- elections, political parties, candidates or movements;
- advocacy for or against religious beliefs or movements; and
- exploitation of current sensitive events or encouragement of unrest.

### 8.6. Gambling

- sports betting;
- casinos, lotteries, bingo or games of chance involving money or prizes; and
- betting tips, odds or forecasts.

### 8.7. Deceptive or harmful financial services

- predatory or risky short-term lending;
- get-rich-quick offers;
- pyramid schemes or misleading multi-level marketing;
- guaranteed investment returns or claimed insider information; and
- hidden fees or material paid-product terms.

### 8.8. Uncertified medicine, treatment or supplements

- unlicensed drugs or supplements;
- medical sales by unauthorized entities;
- unlicensed devices, treatments or prescriptions; and
- unverified health, treatment, weight-loss or weight-gain claims.

A license may not by itself guarantee acceptance; Telegram makes the final decision.

### 8.9. Drugs, alcohol and tobacco

- psychoactive or recreational drugs;
- alcoholic beverages and home-brewing equipment;
- cigarettes, e-cigarettes and tobacco; and
- related paraphernalia or production instructions.

### 8.10. Weapons and explosives

- firearms, melee weapons and harmful sprays;
- ammunition, parts and accessories;
- bombs, grenades, fireworks and firecrackers; and
- acquisition, assembly or production instructions.

### 8.11. Spam, malware and hacking

- unsolicited communication or abusive mass-messaging tools;
- phishing or credential theft;
- malware, viruses or dangerous modified apps;
- unauthorized device or account access;
- fake or automated clicks, traffic, followers or metrics; and
- incentivized artificial actions.

### 8.12. Products of questionable legality

- forged IDs, passports, visas or official documents;
- human trafficking or marriage brokering;
- stolen data, carding or leaked credentials;
- body parts or bodily fluids; and
- counterfeit goods, inauthentic digital goods or counterfeit currency.

## 9. Supporting evidence

Support or Telegram may request licenses, brand ownership evidence, authorization, age classification or legal-status documentation. The order may remain paused until the evidence is reviewed.

Submitting evidence does not guarantee approval.

## 10. Review outcomes

- Changes requested: the current revision cannot be submitted; a reason and correction guidance are provided.
- Submitted to Telegram: the ad has been entered and is awaiting Telegram review.
- Telegram approved: the ad is accepted, but delivery still depends on timing, budget and auctions.
- Telegram rejected: the available reason is shown and the user may create a corrected revision.

No “1–19 days” or other number may be presented as a binding Telegram review commitment.

## 12. Policy changes

Telegram may update its rules. The current version of this Policy and links to the official rules are made available before each order. Material changes may require renewed acceptance.
